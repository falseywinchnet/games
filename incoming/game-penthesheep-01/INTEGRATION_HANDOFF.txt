GAME PENTHESHEEP 01 — Pen the Sheep, a new game for the collection

The classic hex trapping puzzle (the user's "Block the Pig"), with a sheep instead of a pig at the user's request. One of the five games the user asked for on 2026-10-02. It was built on the Neo by Claude Opus, working with the user, borrowing the Eggy, Switchbox and Four Pegs techniques (software 3D, crisp text layers, synthesized audio). The user approved it for integration on 2026-10-02. It replaces nothing: it needs a new collection entry.

A sheep stands in the middle of a hex meadow and wants out. Click a patch of grass to put up a fence; each new fence joins the fences beside it with rails, so walls build themselves. The sheep then trots one patch toward the edge, turning unhurried to face the patch it means to take next. The moment it reaches any edge patch, from any legal direction, it walks straight off the field the way it was heading and is gone. Fence it in so no way leads out and it sits down and sulks, then dozes off: you win. Three fences come free before it starts. There is no fence round the meadow; old, weathered panels already stand here and there, and new fences join onto them.

Sheep and meadows: a dozy sheep dawdles now and then; a clever one takes the shortest way with the most routes onward; a cunning one asks of every step where you'd put your next fence. Any sheep stops for clover (from meadow 5), spending a turn munching. Meadows are endless, generated in the background as you play, from 9x9 with plenty of old fences and dozy sheep to 11x11 with fewer fences and cunning sheep. The sheep is deterministic, so a meadow is only accepted once the game's own shepherd (a solver bot that judges positions by the fewest fences that would still wall the sheep in, a max-flow cut, against how soon it can reach the edge) has penned it; its fence count is the par. Three stars at or under par, two within three fences, one for any win; a hint caps the meadow at two. Undo, restart, hint (hovering Hint traces the sheep's shortest way out), and a Meadows map to replay any meadow.

Music: the user asked for the original game's music to be STUDIED and continued, "so similar it feels like a continuation". With the user's permission the original's two loops were downloaded on 2026-10-02 for study only; they stayed in a scratch folder on the Neo and are not in this package, and nothing was sampled. The study found a melody-less, beatless, shimmering wash of soft, nearly pure tones over a held low C, chorused and very wide, with birdsong. "Pasture Haze" (sh_music, 48 s, odd meadows) follows that recipe with new material and keeps it consonant at the user's direction ("heavenly", not uneasy): five-voice tones spread about +/-14 cents drifting slowly, pentatonic only (C D E G A), octave and fifth doublings over a soft C and G bass, an airy reverb, wide stereo; it matches the original's frequency balance, stereo width and mix of intervals. "Long Grass" (sh_music_grass, 64 s, even meadows) is an earlier, more melodic piece in the same key and spirit that the user asked to keep. Plus a penned tune, a got-away tune, baas, hops, munching, fence knocks.

Package: about 8 MB of sources, runtime audio and screenshots. No app bundle. The WAV music masters stay on the Neo under /Users/ultimussecundai/solitaire_sounds/sheep/audio_src/masters; re-render them with source/audio_src.

CODE BOUNDARY
  Everything is in namespace sh, so it clashes with none of games, eggy, sbx, fp, ap, pt, ld.
  Portable C++20 core (library sh_core):
    field.*      the meadow, the sheep's choice and step, fences, the max-flow cut, the solver bot, generation with proof (pure)
    pasture.*    the scene: hex patches, field, trees, fences that join with rails (old and new), the sheep, clover, picking
    platform/raster|r3d|mesh   the shared renderer
  View, still portable C++ against GUI.Forms:
    sheep_view.*   input, the sheep's turn and walk-off, results, hint, undo, the Meadows map, background generation (std::async), autosave
  macOS-only adapters:
    platform/text.mm     the text.hpp interface (CoreText; Georgia, Menlo)
    platform/audio.mm    the audio.hpp interface (AVFoundation)
    platform/present.mm  NativePresenter (Core Animation), plus dev_resize_window (dev only)
  main.cpp is the standalone entry point. Do not link it into Games.

INTEGRATING INTO THE CABINET
  1. Copy source/src to vendor/penthesheep/src and leave this package unchanged. Build sh_core plus an sh_ui library and link both into game_ui.
  2. Add a new collection entry (a new slot and kind; nothing is replaced). Host
       make_control<sh::SheepView>(gf::StableId("sheep.view"), sh::Options{})
     below the rail like the other vendor views. Call activate() when shown. Call set_cabinet(foreground, music, sound) on tab changes and when the master switches change: it gates its own music and sound, idles at 2 ticks/s when not in front, and stops its music. Stop the cabinet's music while it is in front.
  3. Pointer coordinates: on_pointer() treats e.position as relative to the control (convert under the rail, as for Eggy).
  4. Assets: add the sh_* files and sheep_audio_manifest.json to assets/audio. The ui_name_* files already exist in the cabinet as AAC.
  5. Saves: pen_the_sheep-v1.txt in the cabinet's save folder; GAMES_STATE_DIR redirects it (--dev uses pen_the_sheep-dev-v1.txt). Versioned, checksummed, atomically replaced: the meadow you're on, the furthest reached, the fences you've placed in order, and stars per meadow.
  6. Size: the layout reflows from 600 x 370 points (the cabinet's 600 x 420 less its 50-point rail) to full screen. Below 420 x 300 game pixels the bars slim down, buttons fit their labels (shorter labels when tight), the help card's words step down a size, the Meadows map pages, and the result card fits its words. Rendering is 2 points per game pixel at any size.
  7. Presentation: as with Eggy, Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe. SH_NO_NATIVE=1 forces the LiveSurface fallback. While a panel is open no scene text is drawn.
  8. Keys: Z undo, R restart, H hint, L meadows, M music, F1 help, Esc closes.
  9. Dev only, with --dev: SH_SCRIPT="t:code,..." replays inputs (c<cell> fence, bot (the solver's fence), solve (the proof's next fence), far (a careless fence), hint, undo, help, map, close, next, lvl<n>, size<W>x<H>). SH_WINDOW=WxH opens at a given size. SH_PRINT_WID=1 prints the window id; with it, drawing continues while the window is covered (for captures).

WINDOWS (Shadow)
  These follow the Eggy, Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe seams in docs/WINDOWS_HANDOFF.md.
  - Replace text.mm, audio.mm, present.mm. NativePresenter::attach() may return false to use the LiveSurface fallback. dev_resize_window is a dev aid; a no-op is fine.
  - Two music loops (audio_music crossfades between them by meadow). AAC decoding must honour the encoder priming: decoded lengths must equal loop_end_sample_exclusive (2,304,000 and 3,072,000), as they do through AVAudioFile.
  - Fonts: Georgia and Menlo; on Windows Georgia is present and Consolas can stand in for Menlo.
  - M_PI needs _USE_MATH_DEFINES on MSVC. The -Wall -Wextra flags are Clang/GCC spelling. Generation runs on std::async. The default save root is macOS Library/Application Support; keep the file name and GAMES_STATE_DIR.
  - platform/raster.cpp includes the accumulate() fix (xa/xb clamped to the row; an intermittent one-float overread found under ASan on 2026-10-02). The repository's vendor copies of Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe predate the fix.

POLICY NOTES
  - Every meadow is winnable: it is only offered after the solver has penned its (deterministic) sheep.
  - The sheep never startles at fences; it shows its intention by turning to face its next patch.
  - All audio is synthesized: no samples, no third-party audio. The original game's music was studied, never sampled or shipped.
