GAME LIARSDICE 01 — Liar's Dice, a new game for the collection

Liar's Dice in Davy Jones' locker, one of the five games the user asked for on 2026-10-02. It was built on the Neo by Claude Opus, working with the user, borrowing the Eggy, Switchbox and Four Pegs techniques (software 3D, crisp text layers, synthesized audio). The user approved it for integration on 2026-10-02. It replaces nothing: it needs a new collection entry.

You drowned and owe the locker a hundred years. You win them back at the dice table in the captain's cabin of a long-sunk ship, under a swinging lantern; behind the players a great stern window shows the Keeper, an enormous anglerfish whose lure glows when he speaks. Before each game the Keeper lays three generated wagers on the barrel: the company (two easy marks up to three of the sharpest), the stakes (1 to 100 years added on a loss, struck off on a win), and something stranger (lose and he keeps your shadow or your singing voice in his jar; win and you take home a pearl the size of your fist, or a lost thing back). The same three stay on offer until one is taken. Debt at zero: you're free, and may play on for trinkets.

The game: five dice under each leather cup; raise the bid ("seven fours") or call the last bid a lie; ones are wild and are never bid. On a call every cup comes up, the dice that count glow one by one with a running tally, and whoever was wrong loses a die into the Keeper's jar. The room darkens and the music turns to a heartbeat when bids climb past what's likely.

The crew: a fixed cast of 32 generated once from their names (four each of crab, octopus, skeleton, grouper, eel, turtle, shark, ghost), each with a bluff rate, nerve, greed, skill at the odds, a speed at catching on to your bluffing, and a faint tell of their own (shown on about half their bluffs and one honest bid in eleven). The logbook keeps your record against each and writes in a tell once you've caught it three times. Danger ratings are measured by tools/ladder (40,000 three-handed games).

Score: "The Locker Waltz" in D minor in two arrangements of the same 24 bars at 132 BPM (calm: concertina, banjo, tuba; tense: heartbeat, low pizzicato, trembling glass), cut between on the beat; stingers for Liar!, the bid standing or falling, a win, a loss and freedom; dice rattles, the jar's plunk, voices per creature kind and for the Keeper.

Package: about 6 MB of sources, runtime audio and screenshots. No app bundle. The WAV music masters stay on the Neo under /Users/ultimussecundai/solitaire_sounds/dice/audio_src/masters; re-render them with source/audio_src.

BUILD (standalone, verified on the Neo)
  Copy assets/ into source/assets first. The dev build reads LD_ASSET_DIR; the bundle copies source/assets to Resources/assets.
  cmake -S source -B build -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=<GUI.Forms paint-port SDK> && cmake --build build -j 8
  ctest --test-dir build      dice_tests: the rules, the cast (32 fixed, every kind-and-tell pair unique), the odds model against
                              simulation, 1,500 crew matches (always legal; skill shows; tells correlate with bluffs),
                              adaptation to a heavy bluffer, the wagers, the ledger round trip
  build/ladder               re-measures the crew's danger ratings
  build/preview              headless stills of the cabin

CODE BOUNDARY
  Everything is in namespace ld, so it clashes with none of games, eggy, sbx, fp, ap, pt.
  Portable C++20 core (library ld_core):
    rules.*      bids, raises, calls, the count, matches (pure)
    brain.*      the 32-strong cast, their minds (odds by DP, reading the player, bluffing, tells), baked danger ratings
    wager.*      the Keeper's generated wagers
    crew.*       the creature models and their tells
    cabin.*      the scene: the sunken cabin, the Keeper's window, table, cups, dice, the jar, picking
    save.*       the Ledger (debt, jar, trophies, logbook, the match in progress) and its file
    platform/raster|r3d|mesh   the shared renderer
  View, still portable C++ against GUI.Forms:
    dice_view.*   input, wagers, turns, the reveal, speech, panels, autosave
  macOS-only adapters:
    platform/text.mm     the text.hpp interface (CoreText; Georgia, Menlo)
    platform/audio.mm    the audio.hpp interface (AVFoundation, including the on-the-beat switch between arrangements)
    platform/present.mm  NativePresenter (Core Animation), plus dev_resize_window (dev only)
  main.cpp is the standalone entry point. Do not link it into Games.

INTEGRATING INTO THE CABINET
  1. Copy source/src to vendor/liarsdice/src and leave this package unchanged. Build ld_core plus an ld_ui library and link both into game_ui.
  2. Add a new collection entry (a new slot and kind; nothing is replaced). Host
       make_control<ld::DiceView>(gf::StableId("liarsdice.view"), ld::Options{})
     below the rail like the other vendor views. Call activate() when shown. Call set_cabinet(foreground, music, sound) on tab changes and when the master switches change: it gates its own music and sound, idles at 2 ticks/s when not in front, and stops its music. Stop the cabinet's music while it is in front.
  3. Pointer coordinates: on_pointer() treats e.position as relative to the control (convert under the rail, as for Eggy).
  4. Assets: add the ld_* files and dice_audio_manifest.json to assets/audio. The ui_name_* files already exist in the cabinet as AAC.
  5. Music: two arrangements of the same bars (ld_music, ld_music_tense) share one loop length; the view asks for a switch and audio_music_on_bar (audio.mm) cuts to the other arrangement exactly on the next bar line, with bar lengths from the manifest (bar_samples). This is the same bar-boundary behaviour Four Pegs needs from the shared transport.
  6. Saves: liars_dice-v1.txt in the cabinet's save folder; GAMES_STATE_DIR redirects it (--dev uses liars_dice-dev-v1.txt). Versioned, checksummed, atomically replaced, with any game in progress (dice and all) kept, and the wagers on offer kept so quitting doesn't reshuffle them.
  7. Size: the layout reflows from 600 x 370 points (the cabinet's 600 x 420 less its 50-point rail) to full screen. Under 520 game pixels wide (1040 points) the bid panel becomes two rows (your dice and Liar! above, the composer below), Help/Music/Log move to small buttons in a top band that also carries the bid in one line, and the camera frames the table beneath the band; name plates shrink to the gap between seats. When the three wager cards' words don't fit side by side, the wagers become three tabs over one card. Help and records step their words down a size to fit; the logbook pages. Rendering is 2 points per game pixel at any size.
  8. Presentation: as with Eggy, Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe. LD_NO_NATIVE=1 forces the LiveSurface fallback. While a panel is open no scene text is drawn.
  9. Keys: 2-6 choose a face, Up/Down (or +/-) the number, Enter bids, L (or Backspace) calls liar, M music, F1 help, Esc closes.
  10. Dev only, with --dev: LD_SCRIPT="t:code,..." replays inputs (w<0-2> take a wager, skip, bid, call, auto (you play yourself), speed<k>, help, log, rec, close, again, owed<years>, size<W>x<H>). LD_WINDOW=WxH opens at a given size. LD_PRINT_WID=1 prints the window id; with it, drawing continues while the window is covered (for captures).

WINDOWS (Shadow)
  These follow the Eggy, Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe seams in docs/WINDOWS_HANDOFF.md.
  - Replace text.mm, audio.mm, present.mm. NativePresenter::attach() may return false to use the LiveSurface fallback. dev_resize_window is a dev aid; a no-op is fine.
  - AAC decoding must honour the encoder priming: each arrangement's decoded length must equal loop_end_sample_exclusive (1,570,896), as it does through AVAudioFile, or the on-beat switch drifts.
  - Fonts: Georgia and Menlo; on Windows Georgia is present and Consolas can stand in for Menlo.
  - M_PI needs _USE_MATH_DEFINES on MSVC. The -Wall -Wextra flags are Clang/GCC spelling. The default save root is macOS Library/Application Support; keep the file name and GAMES_STATE_DIR.
  - platform/raster.cpp includes the accumulate() fix (xa/xb clamped to the row; an intermittent one-float overread found under ASan on 2026-10-02). The repository's vendor copies of Switchbox, Four Pegs and Atom Probe predate the fix.

POLICY NOTES
  - Rules commit before animation: bids and calls are recorded (and saved) at once; the show catches up.
  - The crew never see your dice; their reads use only what's on the table and your history of bids.
  - All audio is synthesized: no samples, no third-party audio.

MEASURED / VERIFIED ON THE NEO
  - Clean build of this package's sources: OK (Release, arm64). ctest: PASS. Crew win rates by danger 1/2/3: 0.293 / 0.331 / 0.370 against a fair share of 0.333.
  - Audio: 51/51 files decode; both arrangements' AAC valid-frame counts equal the manifest loop end exactly.
  - The package-built app was driven by LD_SCRIPT in single runs at the default size and resized live to 600 x 370: the wagers (cards and tabs), the crew bidding, your move, reveals with counting, records, the logbook (paged), help. Captured in screens/. Earlier builds were also run under ASan.
  - The user played earlier builds.

NOT VERIFIED
  - In two-row mode the reveal's verdict line ("Seven sixes? There are four. A lie!") was moved to the foot of the scene, clear of the name plates, after the captures; it was not caught on screen at 600 x 370 in that position.
  - Listening: the checks on the score and effects were numerical (levels, no clipping, clean loop seams, equal lengths).
  - Reduced motion: not implemented. The lantern swings and a reveal's room darkening are always on.
  - CPU: not measured. It runs at 30 fps in front, 10 fps in the background, 2 ticks/s when hidden.
  - Cabinet integration and any non-macOS platform.
