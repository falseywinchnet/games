GAME KOIKOI 01 — Koi-Koi, a fifth card game for the card table

The hanafuda game Koi-Koi, one of the five games the user asked for on 2026-10-02, to be "added to the card-game set", building on the cabinet's card engine. It was built on the Neo by Claude Opus, working with the user. The user approved it for integration on 2026-10-02. It replaces nothing.

It could not be compiled into PlaySuite on the Neo: the repository now builds with LLVM 20 and CPU Skia, which are not installed there. So it arrives as a package: the portable rules module, the 48-card deck art, the audio, and a complete Mac table (kk::KoiView) that plays the whole game today on the cabinet's own felt and card finish. The user's earlier feedback on that table ("use the same background generator for the felt"; "cards could use a bit of depth... texture, shading, thickness, just a bit"; the felt must be there on the first frame, as on the other card tables) is built in.

The game: 48 cards, four for each month, each month a flower (pine, plum, cherry, wisteria, iris, peony, bush clover, pampas grass, chrysanthemum, maple, willow, paulownia). Play a card from your hand; if a field card shares its month you take both (choose when two match; take all four when three do), otherwise it joins the field; then the top of the draw pile turns over and is matched the same way. The moment you make a new set: stop and score the round, or call koi-koi and play on (if the opponent then makes a set and stops, they score double). Rounds worth seven or more are doubled; four of a month in the starting hand, or four pairs, wins outright for six. A match is twelve rounds, January to December; the round's winner deals the next. Sets: Five Brights 10, Four Brights 8, Rainy Four Brights 7, Three Brights 5, Viewing the Blossoms 5, Viewing the Moon 5, Boar-Deer-Butterflies 5+, Animals 1+, Red Poem Ribbons 5+, Blue Ribbons 5+, both 10+, Ribbons 1+, Plains 1+ (the Sake Cup counts as a plain too).

Opponents, one per match in turn: Grandmother Hana (careful; stops while ahead), Captain Ishida (calls koi-koi constantly), Little Momo (chases brights), Master Kiri (the strongest: she plays each choice out sixty times against guesses at the hidden cards, every choice against the same guesses, and weighs koi-koi the same way; about 59% against the plain computer over 396 matches; hints use her eye; about 12 ms a move on the Neo).

Art: tools/make_hanafuda.py draws the 48 faces and a back, deterministically, at 360x504 like the collection's other cards: the house's cream border, a hanafuda painting in flat traditional colours, and a small label (month numeral, flower, kind) for learning the deck. Nothing is borrowed or generated by image models.

Score: "Twelve Months", koto and bamboo flute in the yo pentatonic, 32 bars at 66 BPM (about 116 s); effects: the slap of a hanafuda card, captures, a shuffle, a koto flourish for sets, a taiko for koi-koi, wooden clappers for stopping, win and lose phrases.

Package: about 21 MB (13 MB of it screenshots of the felt, which doesn't compress). No app bundle. The WAV masters stay on the Neo under /Users/ultimussecundai/solitaire_sounds/koikoi/audio_src/masters; re-render them with source/audio_src/koikoi_audio.py.

BUILD (standalone, verified on the Neo)
  Copy assets/ into source/assets first. The dev build reads KK_ASSET_DIR; the bundle copies source/assets to Resources/assets.
  cmake -S source -B build -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=<GUI.Forms paint-port SDK> && cmake --build build -j 8
  ctest --test-dir build      koikoi_tests (about 40 s): the deck, every set, the quick count agreeing with the named sets on 20,000
                              random piles, the doublings and the deal, matching and choosing, 700 whole matches, the temperaments,
                              Master Kiri beating the plain computer, saving

INTEGRATING INTO THE CABINET
  Recommended: host the table as the fifth card game.
  1. Copy src/koikoi.hpp/.cpp beside game.cpp (namespace games::koi; it uses nothing else of the cabinet) and add koikoi_tests to the test suite. Copy the view to vendor/koikoi/src and leave this package unchanged.
  2. Do NOT compile the package's src/paint/* into game_ui: it is a copy of vendor/paint and would duplicate paint::render_carpet_tile and friends. Link the cabinet's own; KoiView calls paint::render_carpet_tile(paint::carpet_preset(3), 256) exactly as the card table does. src/card_finish.cpp is a verbatim copy of the card_finish that table.cpp keeps in an anonymous namespace, so the two don't collide at link time; better still, move it to one shared place and use it from both.
  3. Put Koi-Koi on the card shelf with Solitaire, Spider, FreeCell and Hearts. The card table's Kind/State model is built round 52-card piles and doesn't fit a matching game, so host
       make_control<kk::KoiView>(gf::StableId("koikoi.view"), kk::Options{})
     for that shelf entry rather than adding a Kind. Call activate() when shown. Call set_cabinet(foreground, music, sound) on tab changes and when the master switches change: it gates its own music and sound, idles at 2 ticks/s when not in front, and stops its music. Stop the cabinet's music while it is in front.
     (Alternative: draw Koi-Koi inside table.cpp as a new Kind using games::koi for the rules and KoiView as the reference for the layout, motion and dialogs. That is more work and was not attempted here.)
  4. Pointer coordinates: on_pointer() treats e.position as relative to the control (convert under the rail if it sits below one).
  5. Assets: add assets/cards/hana_*.png to the cabinet's card art and the kk_* audio and koikoi_audio_manifest.json to assets/audio. card_shadow.png and the ui_name_* files already exist in the cabinet.
  6. Saves: koikoi-v1.txt in the cabinet's save folder; GAMES_STATE_DIR redirects it (--dev uses koikoi-dev-v1.txt). Checksummed, atomically replaced: the whole match in progress (hands, field, pile, captures, koi-koi calls, the twelve months so far), whose turn the next opponent is, and the music/sound switches. A file whose state fails the invariant is refused.
  7. Size: the layout reflows from 600 x 370 points (the cabinet's 600 x 420 less its 50-point rail) to full screen. From 900 x 560 up the scoreboard runs down the right side; below that a slim top bar carries the score, the month, the opponent and the buttons (Hint, Rules, Sets, Score, Music), the twelve-month record moves to a Score panel, and the cards size to the space. Rules and sets step their words down a size to fit; dialogs and banners fit the table. Card art is reduced from the 360x504 masters to the drawn size on resize.
  8. Felt: woven synchronously before the first frame (about 1 s on the Neo), as the card table does on attach, so the felt is there at once.
  9. Presentation: a Core Animation layer at absolute_bounds (KK_NO_NATIVE=1 forces the LiveSurface fallback); text is drawn into the frame at physical resolution.
  10. Keys: 1-8 play a card, H hint, K koi-koi, S stop, Enter continues, M music, F1 rules, Esc closes.
  11. Dev only, with --dev: KK_SCRIPT="t:code,..." replays inputs (p<i> play hand card i, best (the computer's choice for you), koi, stop, hint, next, auto (you play yourself), speed<k>, rules, sets, score, close, size<W>x<H>). KK_WINDOW=WxH opens at a given size. KK_PRINT_WID=1 prints the window id; with it, drawing continues while the window is covered (for captures).

WINDOWS (Shadow)
  - Replace text.mm, audio.mm, present.mm and image.mm. For load_png use gf::Window::load_png, as table.cpp does for its 360x504 card PNGs, and convert the result to the view's premultiplied BGRA Canvas; the area-average reduce in image.mm is plain C++ and can move to a .cpp. NativePresenter::attach() may return false to use the LiveSurface fallback. dev_resize_window is a dev aid; a no-op is fine.
  - Music is one loop. AAC decoding must honour the encoder priming: the decoded length must equal loop_end_sample_exclusive (5,585,455), as it does through AVAudioFile.
  - Fonts: Georgia and Menlo at run time; on Windows Georgia is present and Consolas can stand in for Menlo.
  - The -Wall -Wextra flags are Clang/GCC spelling. The default save root is macOS Library/Application Support; keep the file name and GAMES_STATE_DIR.
  - Authoring only: tools/make_hanafuda.py (Python/Pillow) uses macOS Georgia and, for the one 寿 on the sake cup, Hiragino Mincho. The finished PNGs are in the package; regenerating elsewhere needs appropriately licensed local fonts (as with tools/make_cards.py).

POLICY NOTES
  - The computer players never look at your hand or the pile: Master Kiri's foresight samples guesses consistent with what she can see.
  - Rules commit before animation; the show catches up (a played card lands on its match before both go to the captures).
  - All audio is synthesized: no samples, no third-party audio. The card art is drawn by the package's own script.

MEASURED / VERIFIED ON THE NEO
  - Clean build of this package's sources: OK (Release, arm64). ctest: PASS. In the test run: 2,400 computer rounds, 61 drawn; a careless player wins 7 of 200 matches; Captain Ishida calls koi-koi 573 times to Grandmother Hana's 23; Master Kiri beats the plain computer 63 to 54 (and wins 233 of 396 matches in a longer run).
  - Audio: 16/16 files decode; the music's AAC valid-frame count equals the manifest loop end exactly.
  - The package-built app was driven by KK_SCRIPT in single runs at the default size and resized live to 600 x 370: the koi-koi decision (from a saved mid-round state, which also exercised resume), koi-koi called, sets made, a choice between two matches, a round's end, the rules, the sets, the Score panel. Captured in screens/ (two_match_take_which.png and round_over.png come from the same build before the resizing work; the default-size layout is unchanged).
  - The user played earlier builds and directed the felt, the card depth and the first-frame felt.

NOT VERIFIED
  - Listening: the checks on the score and effects were numerical (levels, no clipping, a clean loop seam).
  - Reduced motion: not implemented. Cards always glide.
  - CPU: not measured beyond Kiri's move time. It runs at 60 fps in front, 10 fps in the background, 2 ticks/s when hidden.
  - Cabinet integration (including the link against the cabinet's own paint and card_finish) and any non-macOS platform.
