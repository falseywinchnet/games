Heat talk, heat talk. Things I say when it is hot.
Do not listen to the heat. It makes up silly stuff.
Like that rocks can talk. Or that the sun is mad.
The sun is not mad. It is just very, very shiny.
I am not mad either. I am hot. There is a difference.
Hot and happy. Hot and hopeful. Hot and Eggy.
At last, a long, cool shadow from a cliff. I dive in.
Splash! Not splash. Plop. I plop into the shade.
Ah. Ah. Ah. Ah. The best four ahs of the day.
I lie on my back and look at the underside of the cliff.
It is full of tiny holes and bumps and lines. A cool ceiling.
I could lie here and read it all day. It is a good book.
It is a rock book. Rock stories are slow but sure.
I read one line. Stone. Stone. Stone. Fine story.
My eyes close. My breathing slows. I am cool at last.
A drop falls from the ceiling onto my beak. Plink.
Ha! The cliff is crying with joy. Or sweating. Like me.
It is a sweaty cliff. We are the same.
Sweaty cliff, sweaty Eggy. A tiny team of the hot.
Thank you, cliff. You are cool and wise.
The sun is going down now. The air is cooling. Sigh.
The best part of a hot day is when it gets less hot.
The second best part is the first sip of water.
The third is the shade.
All three, today. A good day. A hot, but good day.
Sunlit summit ahead! It glows like a lantern.
The peak is so bright it looks like a candle flame.
I climb toward the glow. Warm. Brighter. Warmer.
Top of the world, top of the world, here I come.
The sun is closer here. I can feel it on my whole self.
It is gentle at the summit. Not a heat, a hug.
The light on the peaks is pink and gold, like melted sherbet.
I do not know sherbet. It sounds cold and sweet.
Perfect for a sunny peak.
I reach out a wing to touch the light. It touches back.
Warm. Warm on my feathers. Warm on my fluff. Warm on my heart.
A duck in a lamp's glow.
A glowing, golden duck. Bright as the morning.
All the rocks have turned to gold. Gold ridge, gold peak, gold sky.
I am standing in a painting. A big gold painting.
I take a deep breath of golden air. Aaah.
The summit warms my feet. Nice, flat, warm stone.
I lay down my little acorn helmet beside me. It is gold too.
Look at it shine. I should polish it more often.
I give it a rub with my wing. Shine, shine, shine.
It is the shiniest acorn of all the acorns. The king of acorns.
I put it back on my head. A golden crown. Just for now.
King of the Sunlit Peak. Population: me.
I rule fairly. I give the rocks a day off.
Rocks may bask. Peaks may glow. Winds may rest.
It is an order from the king. And the king is very warm.
Hello, sky. You are so wide. So blue. So friendly.
The sun smiles. I smile back. A warm smile.
I did it. I climbed to the sunlit place. My feet are tired and happy.
A warm breeze ruffles my feathers. Lovely. Not too hot.
Just right. At last. This is it. The just-right spot.
I told myself I would find it. And I did. Oh, I did.
Now I will sit here and let the sun soak in.
A sun-soak, a warm wind, a long, slow sigh.
I am so happy my eyes are watering. Warm tears, golden tears.
They roll down my beak and glitter. Little suns.
I wipe them with my wing. It is just the light.
It is just the light being very bright and very kind.
I could sit here for a thousand years. But there is more mountain.
There is always more mountain. That is the best part.
But for now, here. Warm. Safe. Golden. Round.
Goodbye, sun-soaked peak. I will carry your light in my feathers.
Onward, up. The sun on my back like a hand pushing me gently.
The sun is behind me, so my shadow leads the way.
Long, thin Shadow Eggy, going up the slope in front of me.
Follow the shadow, follow the shadow, it knows the way.
It does not know. It is only a shadow. But it looks sure.
I like a shadow that looks sure. It makes me feel sure.
We climb together. Two Eggys, one gold, one dark.
Up, up, up, into the warm, warm light.
A heat haze hangs over the valley like thin gold gauze.
I peek down at it from a height. It is like a sea of light.
A sea I can look at but not drown in. Perfect.
I wave to the haze. It waves back, wobbling. Bye.
The sun is high again. The rocks are sizzling. Hup!
My toes dance on the hot stone. Hop, hop, hop.
I find a crack of shade and leap into it. Safe!
Safe in a crack. Safe in the cool. Safe from the sun.
The sun peeks in. It wants to find me. Go away, sun.
It does not go away. It slides slowly across the crack.
I slide with it, deeper in. A game of sun hide-and-seek.
I am winning. The sun is a poor seeker. Too bright to look closely.
Hide, Eggy, hide! I tuck behind a stone.
The sun passes. I peek out. Safe. I win. Hup hup!
Hooray for me. Hooray for shade. Hooray for stone.
Hooray for the whole hot, wonderful, silly mountain.
