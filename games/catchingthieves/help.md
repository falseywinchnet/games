Push a pumpkin onto every burrow to keep the raccoon bandits out of the bear’s garden. The bear can push one pumpkin when the square beyond it is clear. He cannot pull. A pumpkin pushed into a corner, or against a hedge with no burrow along it, is stuck for good; Undo takes it back.

Arrow keys or WASD walk. Click an empty square to walk there, or a pumpkin beside the bear to push it. Z, U or Backspace undo; R starts this garden over. Hint points to a useful next push. H or F1 opens Help.

Next in the bar chooses the difficulty of the next garden: Tutorial, Easy, Medium or Hard. Tutorial gardens are short lessons, each showing one idea, dealt at random. Easy to Hard gardens get bigger, hold more pumpkins and need longer, more careful solutions. If the garden in play is untouched, a new difficulty replaces it at once; otherwise it applies to the next garden.

New garden deals a fresh garden at the chosen difficulty. Every garden has a verified solution. Finish one to keep a bronze pumpkin, come close to its fewest pushes for silver, or match it for gold.

Every committed move is saved. Reopening resumes the same garden, pumpkins and undo history. The suite’s Music, Sound and Motion switches apply here too. With reduced motion, moves and their results appear immediately.
