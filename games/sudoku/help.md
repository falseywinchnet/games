Fill every row, column and 3 × 3 box with the digits 1–9, once each. Choose a digit from the column beside the board and click a cell to place it; right-click to add a note. The × beneath the digits is the eraser: choose it, then click a cell to clear it. Given digits cannot be changed.

Arrows move the selection, number keys enter digits, N toggles notes, Backspace erases and Z undoes. Mistakes remain counted after undo. Hover a filled square to highlight its digit, row and column.

The puzzle saves after each change. Difficulty selects the next puzzle; New game creates a unique puzzle offline. Top scores keep ten names per difficulty, ranked by fewest mistakes.
