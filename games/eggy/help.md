Eggy is climbing the Very, Very Tall Mountain. It is very, very tall. Arrow keys or W A S D steer, with Up pointing uphill; Space hops. You can also hold the pointer on the ground to guide him.

Stop helping and he climbs by himself, a little slower. He never gives up and keeps climbing while the game is closed. Ice is slippery: look for stone ledges. Hop logs and rocks. Leaves can bonk him, wind slows him and water refreshes him. Floating stars require a helping hand; the autopilot will not chase them.

T opens top scores; + and − change zoom. Why can't Eggy be your screensaver? Because the ducks always find their way onto people's desks.
