# Koi-Koi

The hanafuda game, made for PlaySuite's card table as its fifth card game.

## The game

- **The deck:** 48 cards, four for each month, each month a flower: pine,
  plum, cherry, wisteria, iris, peony, bush clover, pampas grass,
  chrysanthemum, maple, willow, paulownia.
- **A turn:** play a card from your hand.
  - If a field card shares its month, you take both. Choose which when two
    match; take all four when three do.
  - Otherwise your card joins the field.
  - Then the top of the draw pile turns over and is matched the same way.
- **Sets:** the moment you make a new set, choose:
  - **Stop:** score the round.
  - **Koi-koi:** play on for more. If your opponent then makes a set and
    stops, they score double.
- **Scoring:** rounds worth seven or more are doubled. Four of a month in the
  starting hand, or four pairs, wins outright for six.
- **A match:** twelve rounds, January to December. The winner of a round
  deals the next.
- **The sets:**

  | Set | Points |
  |---|---|
  | Five Brights | 10 |
  | Four Brights | 8 |
  | Rainy Four Brights | 7 |
  | Three Brights | 5 |
  | Viewing the Blossoms | 5 |
  | Viewing the Moon | 5 |
  | Boar, Deer and Butterflies | 5 + 1 per extra animal |
  | Animals | 1 + 1 per extra animal |
  | Red Poem Ribbons | 5 + 1 per extra ribbon |
  | Blue Ribbons | 5 + 1 per extra ribbon |
  | Both poem and blue ribbons | 10 + 1 per extra ribbon |
  | Ribbons | 1 + 1 per extra ribbon |
  | Plains (the Sake Cup counts) | 1 + 1 per extra plain |

## The opponents

Four temperaments, one per match in turn:

- **Grandmother Hana:** careful; stops while she's ahead.
- **Captain Ishida:** calls koi-koi constantly.
- **Little Momo:** chases brights.
- **Master Kiri:** the strongest.
  - She plays each choice out sixty times against guesses at the hidden
    cards, every choice against the same guesses, and weighs koi-koi the same
    way.
  - She beats the plain computer about 59% of the time.
  - Hints use her eye.

## The art and the table

- **The deck:** `tools/make_hanafuda.py` draws the 48 faces and a back,
  deterministically, at 360x504 like the collection's other cards.
  - Each card has the house's cream border, a hanafuda painting in flat
    traditional colours, and a small label (month numeral, flower, kind) for
    learning the deck.
  - Nothing is borrowed or generated by image models.
- **The Mac table** uses the card table's own felt and card finish:
  - **Felt:** `shared/felt`'s carpet, preset 3, under its radial light.
  - **Finish:** `card_finish`, copied verbatim from `src/table.cpp`.
  - **Shadow:** the table's card shadow.
  - **Thickness:** a slim dark-red edge, since hanafuda are thick cards.

## Sound

- **Music:** "Twelve Months", koto and bamboo flute in the yo pentatonic,
  consonant and calm.
- **Effects:** the sharp slap of a hanafuda card, captures, a shuffle, a koto
  flourish for sets, a taiko for koi-koi and wooden clappers for stopping.
- **Synthesized:** everything (`audio_src/koikoi_audio.py`).

## Code

- `src/koikoi.hpp/.cpp`: the rules, the computer players and saving, in
  `games::koi`. They are portable and meant to sit beside `game.cpp` in
  PlaySuite.
- `tests/koikoi_tests.cpp`: tests of:
  - the deck
  - every set, and the fast count agreeing with the named sets on 20,000
    piles
  - the doublings and the deal
  - matching and choosing
  - 700 whole matches
  - the temperaments
  - Master Kiri's strength
  - saving
- `src/koi_view.*`: the Mac table, for playing and testing now.

## Build

```bash
cmake -S . -B build -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=$PWD/../eggy/sdk && cmake --build build -j8
```

- `build/KoiKoi.app` is the game; `build/koikoi_tests` runs the tests.
- The window resizes freely down to 600 x 370 points (the cabinet's 600 x 420
  less its rail) and the layout reflows to fit.
- `--dev` uses a separate save and allows `KK_SCRIPT` (including `size600x370`
  to resize the window mid-run) and `KK_WINDOW=WxH` to open at a given size.
- Saves are `koikoi-v1.txt` under `GAMES_STATE_DIR`.
