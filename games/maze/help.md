Each maze names what it holds as it begins; find it, and enter the room full of it. A few seconds later the next maze begins. There is no end: new maze mechanics arrive gradually and combine in later levels, and every generated maze has a proven route.

Left alone, the maze walks itself, like the screensaver it comes from: a passive player wanders, dawdles, stops for anyone it meets and gets there in the end. Press a key or click the view to take over. Leave it for twenty seconds and it carries on from wherever you stopped. It walks only while the window is in use, and stands still under reduced motion.

Up or W walks forward; Down or S steps back; Left/A and Right/D turn. Click the left or right third to turn, the middle to walk, or the lower middle to step back.

Step on a colored pad to open its matching door. Slightly unusual carpet may hide a portal. An elevator connects the two floors. Touching a flip stone puts you on the ceiling: floor pads and elevators become unreachable, and ceiling pads become available. Light bulbs toggle the blackout.

The marble can block your way but will roll away. The paint snail paints the floors, walls and ceilings. Corridor visitors may speak or help. Restart maze keeps the same maze; the maze you are on saves locally, and a reopened maze starts from its entrance.

The command bar offers Restart maze and Who's who. Music, Sound and Motion obey PlaySuite's master switches. Reduced motion snaps the camera instead of gliding, bobbing, rolling or flashing, and keeps the passive player still; moving inhabitants continue to follow the same rules.
