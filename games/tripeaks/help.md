Clear the three peaks. Click an open card one rank higher or lower than the waste card to play it there; a king and an ace are next to each other, so a run can go round the corner. A card turns over when both cards resting on it are gone.

Click the stock to turn its next card onto the waste. The stock is turned only once, so look along the peaks for the longest run before you turn it.

Each card of a run is worth one point more than the last, and sounds a step higher up the scale. Turning the stock ends the run. The top of a peak is worth 15 points, and clearing all three earns 50 more and 10 for every card still in the stock.

Arrows choose an open card or the stock, Enter plays it, Space turns the stock, Z undoes and H hints. Every deal is shuffled fresh and proven winnable before it reaches you; Hint follows a winning line while there is one. When the last cards of the peaks are face up and can be cleared, Solve? plays them out. Settings (the cog) changes the card back, the hints and the cards' finish.
