A complete king-to-ace run of one suit moves home automatically. Clear eight runs to win. Click the stock to deal one card onto every column; every column must contain a card first. Options offers one, two or four suits and starts a new deal when this rule changes.

Click a card then its destination, or drag a legal run. Arrows select, Enter picks up or places, Space deals, Z undoes. The visible Hint action suggests a move.
