Something has happened in the aviary, and one of the parrots did it. One or two of the birds are liars: everything they say about it is false. Two know the truth and tell it. The rest know nothing and only chatter.

Click a bird to hear it; click again and it may say something else. The birds who are playing come back to the same few facts in new words, while the chatterers talk about the weather, the day, their perches and their jokes. Finding out who is playing is part of the case.

Some claims can be checked by looking: who sits on rope, brass, iron, stone or wood, in shade or sun, high or low, beside whom. On later tables one playing bird will only answer a question, and only one of the questions on offer settles the case.

Mark birds liar, truthful or no help from their popups. When sure, name the culprit. Every table is solvable by reasoning alone. Esc closes a popup; Enter continues. Records shows your results.
