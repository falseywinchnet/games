# Tumbled Stones: game record

## VERIFIED

- `tumbledstones_rules` (design): generation, orientation group and the pictures'
  poses, set-down, swaps and marks, juggles, a 400-move played-out game on six seeds
  (families run out and come in; juggles happen; the tray stays sound and playable),
  save round trip and refusals, shapes' self- and cross-distances. All pass on the M4.
- `tumbledstones_view_contract`: frames, help, 150 percent, 600 x 370 and 600 x 320,
  idle and hidden. Passes on the M4.
- Native standalone script `tests/native.script` on the M4: chooser, begin, a flip
  caught mid-turn, a turn, set down, a juggle in flight, help, 600 x 420, the chooser
  small. Captures inspected.
- Headless previews (`ts_preview`) of every state at 1100 x 760 scale 2 and the small
  sizes, inspected.

## NOT VERIFIED

- The sounds and music have not been listened to by the owner.
- Windows and Linux: left to CI.

## DECISIONS

- The room light is the card tables' room (authoring/table_light/trace.cpp, borrowed
  into tools/bake_room.cpp), traced from the tray with the side glass moved back
  ("window" variant, chosen by the owner over the sunlit one); `src/room_bake.cpp` is
  generated by it.
- Shapes, minerals and the tray were shown as candidates; the owner chose window light
  and bare-wood sockets, asked for narrow walls, and for petrified wood and agate drawn
  from reference photos (tumble-polished Wyoming fossil wood; Lake Superior agates).
- A juggle on request was added (the owner agreed): with free turning, a tray with no
  match left almost never happens.
- The worker crew in the view is the card table's (shared/cards/table_art.cpp),
  borrowed. The synth engine in audio_src/engine is Catching Thieves', borrowed.
- Fibrous minerals (walnut, ebony, petrified wood, tiger's eye) lay their grain along
  the stone's long axis, and agate in a nodule follows its bands: looks only.
