A shelf of books to read. Choose one by clicking its spine, or move along the shelf
with the arrow keys and press Enter. Every book opens where you left it.

Turn the pages with the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, the space bar, the wheel,
or a click on either page. C shows the book's contents; choose a part to go straight
to it. Escape closes the contents, then returns to the shelf. The capsule has Shelf,
Contents, Previous page and Next page. The type size is on the Settings screen.

The books are the Berean Standard Bible, the Holy Scriptures of the Jewish
Publication Society (1917), Gorfinkle's Sayings of the Jewish Fathers (Pirke Abot,
1913), Cohen's Tractate Berakot (1921), Strong's Hebrew and Greek dictionaries, and a
concordance of the Berean Standard Bible by Strong's numbers. Every one is in the
public domain; each is set exactly as its source gives it.
