Build each foundation from ace through king in one suit. In the seven tableau columns, build downward in alternating red and black. Move a correctly ordered face-up run together. Only a king may fill an empty column. Hidden cards turn over automatically when exposed.

Click the stock to turn one card, or three in Draw Three mode. The waste accumulates underneath its visible cards. Only its top card is playable. Recycle the waste by clicking the empty stock; there is no redeal limit. Clear all 52 cards to win.

Click a card then its destination, or drag a legal run. Double-click sends a card to a foundation. Gold outlines show selection and legal destinations. Arrows choose a pile/card, Enter selects or places, Space draws, Z undoes. Use Hint for a suggested legal move; a hint is not a promise of a win. Undo retains the last 512 actions. Options changes the card back and deal rules.
