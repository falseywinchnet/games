Light every lamp. Pressing a lamp changes it and its horizontal and vertical
neighbors. Every generated board can be solved. Your current board saves
automatically; choose New board for a fresh puzzle or Undo to reverse a press.

Click a lamp, or move the selection with the arrow keys and press Enter or Space.

Music, Sound and Motion switches apply here. H or F1 opens help; Escape closes it.
