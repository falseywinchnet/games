Click a gem then a neighbor to swap them, or drag the gem itself. Make a line of three or more of the same color. A swap without a match slides back. Wait and an available pair will nudge.

Four in a line makes a colored bomb; matching it clears a 3 × 3 area. A T or L match makes a star; matching it clears its row and column. Five in a line makes a hypercube; swap it with a color to clear that color.

Blasts trigger other specials. Cascades refill the board from above. Additional colors arrive as you progress. The run ends when no swap is available.
