Rebuild the blue and yellow design using every piece. Drag a piece onto the frame; it snaps into place.

While carrying a piece, R or a right-click turns it, and F mirrors it. Click the selected piece to turn it before dragging. Drag a placed piece to move it; right-click a placed piece to return it.

Choose the next game's difficulty with Next in the command bar. Easy games are short lessons in one idea: turning, mirroring, or slanted cuts. Medium games cut six to eight pieces from a square, octagon, hexagon, house or oblong; some are the classic tangram. Hard games cut nine to twelve pieces, often from larger frames.

Every design is cut from a real tiling, so a solution always exists. Any arrangement that matches the colours also counts.
