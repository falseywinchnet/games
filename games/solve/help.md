Rebuild the blue and yellow picture using all seven pieces. Drag a piece from the tray into the frame; it snaps into place.

While carrying a piece, R or a right-click rotates it, and F mirrors it. Click the selected piece in the tray to turn it before dragging. Drag a placed piece to move it; right-click a placed piece to return it.

The target comes from an actual tiling. An alternative arrangement with the same colors is also a solution.
