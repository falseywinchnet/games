Join each coloured pair of squares with a line across the three faces of the cube. Lines may not cross, share a square or pass a stone. You need not cover every square.

Press a coloured square and trace a line from square to square; it folds over the cube's edges. Trace back over a line to shorten it, or press one of its ends to draw it again. Move the pointer without pressing to tilt the cube.

Portals come in linked pairs, matched by the studs on their rims. A line that steps into a portal comes out of its partner and carries on in any direction; a portal is never an ordinary square. The first line through a pair claims it in its colour until that line is erased.

A board is solved when every pair is joined; the cube spins away and the next one arrives. Easy boards are small. Medium adds stone walls and its first portals. Hard is larger, walled and portalled, and has only one way to join every pair without a detour. The Level setting in Settings chooses the next level. Every board has a solution.

Keys: N deals a new board, H or F1 opens this help, Escape closes the top scores.
